Specialty Treatment

Dental Implants — permanent, natural-looking teeth

Lose a tooth, not your confidence. Our titanium implants last a lifetime and feel completely natural.

01
Free Consultation & X-Ray
Digital X-ray and bone density check to plan your implant precisely — at no extra cost.
02
Titanium Post Placement
Under local anaesthesia, a titanium post is placed into your jawbone — quick and painless.
03
Healing & Bonding
The implant bonds naturally with your bone over 4–8 weeks. Most patients resume normal life the next day.
